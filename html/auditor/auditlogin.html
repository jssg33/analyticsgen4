Sign in

Signs in against your Enterprise API. Your name, role, and audit trail come from that account.

This page POSTs your username and password directly to over HTTPS — nothing else. On success you're redirected to auditviewer.html. If sign-in fails with a network/CORS-looking error rather than a rejected-credentials message, the backend needs this page's origin added to its allowed CORS origins. Your name/role are saved to this browser's storage so auditviewer.html still knows who you are after a reload; your password and session token are never saved anywhere.

For local testing without a live backend: manager / manager signs in without making any network call at all.
